# College Preparation Plan — intake form and design sheet

The student is the client. Every document is signed by the student and can be revoked by the student at any time. Parents and guardians are contacts and nominees.

The package is a durable power of attorney, a health care proxy, a HIPAA authorization, and a living will, plus a FERPA release when the student asks for one and a simple will when the attorney includes one. There is no trust and no deed.

**Required** must be answered before the section is complete. **Recommended** is asked for and flagged if blank. **Optional** may be left blank. A question marked **Discuss** may be deferred with “Discuss at the meeting”; that answer still blocks drafting until the attorney resolves it on the design sheet.

Intake answers land on the design sheet already filled in. Fields marked **Design sheet only** are not on the questionnaire.

### 4. Agent Designations

*Financial agent, health care agent, and who may receive information. About 4 minutes.*

**Durable power of attorney — financial and legal agent**

> At 18 the student gains independence in signing contracts, taking loans and credit cards, and in being sued. A durable power of attorney lets the student give a parent, guardian, or trusted adult authority over financial and legal matters. The student can grant all or some of these rights, and can give different aspects to different people.
>
> “Durable” means the authority survives the student’s incapacity. That is the entire point: a power of attorney that stops working when the person becomes unable to act is a power of attorney that stops working exactly when it is needed.

The student may mark “choose the agents at the meeting” so blank nominations are intentional.

**Financial and legal agent.** Handles the student’s bills, bank accounts, tuition, loans, leases, and disputes. Name a primary agent and one or two alternates. Most students name a parent as primary and the other parent, or an aunt or older sibling, as alternate. Co-agents are allowed. Up to four alternates.

Each nominee: name, relationship, phone, email, and address, chosen from people already entered or typed new.

**Health care proxy — health care agent**

> Once the student turns 18 parents can no longer access medical records or make most medical decisions. Emergency rooms sometimes work around this, but relying on a gray area during the worst hour of your life is not a plan.
>
> In Massachusetts the operative document is the Health Care Proxy. The agent steps in only when a physician determines the student cannot make or communicate a health care decision. Until that determination, the student remains fully in charge.
>
> Massachusetts permits only one health care agent at a time — name a single agent and one alternate, not co-agents.

**Health care agent.** Makes medical decisions if a physician determines the student cannot make or communicate them. Someone who knows the student’s wishes and can be reached quickly. No co-agents.

**HIPAA authorization — who may receive medical information**

> HIPAA requires providers to keep medical details private. In an emergency, though, you may want doctors to be able to share updates with family, or to give family enough information to make informed decisions when the student cannot speak.
>
> A HIPAA authorization is narrower and quieter than a proxy. It grants no decision-making power at all — it simply permits a provider to talk. That is why we usually give HIPAA authority to more people than hold the proxy.

**Everyone a provider should be allowed to talk to.** Repeat. Grandparents, siblings, and a roommate’s parent are common additions.

### 5. Power of Attorney — Scope

*When it takes effect and what it covers. About 2 minutes.*

**When should the power of attorney take effect?** Required. Discuss allowed.

- Immediately upon signing. Recommended — institutions act without demanding proof of incapacity.
- Only upon a documented determination of incapacity. “Springing” — the agent must produce a physician’s determination first.
- Discuss at the meeting.

> A springing power of attorney works once someone produces a physician’s written determination of incapacity. Banks and bursars are cautious, and collecting that determination takes days you may not have. An immediate power of attorney is not a transfer of control — the student keeps every right to act, and can revoke the document at any time.

**Powers granted.** Recommended. Check all that apply. Hidden when the effective date is “Discuss at the meeting”.

- Banking and bank accounts
- Paying bills and routine expenses
- Tuition, bursar, and student accounts
- Financial aid office and scholarships
- Student loans and loan servicers
- Credit cards and consumer debt
- Tax filings and communication with the IRS / DOR
- Leases, housing, and security deposits
- Auto — registration, insurance, repairs
- Insurance claims of any kind
- Retaining and instructing an attorney
- Responding to citations, summonses, or conduct charges
- Investment and brokerage accounts
- Digital accounts and online access
- Employment paperwork and payroll
- Signing contracts on the student’s behalf

### 8. Living Will

*Written instructions about care. About 2 minutes.*

> A living will is a written statement of the care the student would and would not want if they could not speak for themselves. Massachusetts does not give living wills the same statutory force as a health care proxy, but doctors and agents read them, and families rely on them. Its real value is that it takes the weight off the agent. Whoever the student names should never have to guess — or carry a decision alone that the student already made.

**Should the package include a living will?** Required. Discuss allowed.

- Yes — include written treatment instructions
- No — leave every decision to the health care agent
- Discuss at the meeting

The questions below appear only when the answer is yes.

**If there is no reasonable hope of recovery, what should happen?** Required. Discuss allowed.

- Use all available measures to prolong life. Continue life-sustaining treatment regardless of prognosis.
- Comfort care only if there is no reasonable hope of recovery. Withhold or withdraw life-sustaining treatment; keep me comfortable.
- Leave it to my health care agent. No written instruction — the agent decides in the moment.
- Discuss at the meeting.

**Specific treatments.** Each is optional. Discuss allowed.

Choices for each: Yes, I would want it / No, I would not want it / My agent decides / Discuss at the meeting.

- Attempted resuscitation (CPR)
- Artificial nutrition and hydration (feeding tube)
- Mechanical ventilation (breathing machine)

| Provide pain relief and comfort care at all times, even if it may hasten death | Yes / No | Optional | Living will is included |
| Would the student want to donate organs or tissue? | Choice | Recommended | Living will is included |
| Which organs, tissue, or purposes? | Long text | Optional | Donation is “only the organs or purposes I name” |
| Anything else the family and doctors should know | Long text | Optional | Always, even if there is no living will |

Organ donation choices:

- Yes — any needed organs or tissue
- Yes — only the organs or purposes I name below
- No
- Discuss at the meeting

## Design sheet

A College Preparation Plan matter opens these tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Intake Review | Read-only snapshot of the submitted questionnaire, when one exists |
| Student Information | The student’s name, citizenship, and permanent address (the client columns) |
| Agent Designations | Financial agent and health care agent. Personal representative appears once a simple will is included |
| College Preparation Plan | School, FERPA, power-of-attorney scope, HIPAA, living will, and the simple will |
| Financial Power of Attorney | Statutory powers seeded from the student’s power list |
| Health Care & Directives | Proxy, HIPAA, and living-will provisions seeded from the questionnaire |

Trust, estate-plan, asset, and deed tabs are not on this sheet. The plan track is read-only at the top of the College Preparation Plan section, with a link back to the matter.

Deferred client answers (“Discuss at the meeting”) are called out in the section. The attorney resolves them here before drafting.
