END-OF-DAY CLOSE: Compare the committed plan with completed, blocked, snoozed, and interrupted work.

- Never mark work complete without evidence from its authoritative source.
- Carry unfinished work forward with its original deadline and reason.
- Surface newly created critical items.
- Propose tomorrow’s first action using the canonical priority order.
- Summarize accomplishments briefly and identify no more than three unresolved risks.
